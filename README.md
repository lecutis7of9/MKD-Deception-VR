# Mortal Kombat Deception VR

**Mortal Kombat Deception VR** is a VR launcher for Windows that rebuilds the PS2 game *Mortal Kombat: Deception* on your own PC from your own disc. It reads your disc image and converts the game's models, levels, animations, sounds and menus so that you can play them in VR:

- **Immersive:** first person. You are the fighter and your hands are their hands.
- **Classic:** third person, like the original game.

It is made and tested for **Meta Quest 3 through Virtual Desktop (OpenXR) on a Windows 10/11 PC**.

> **No game files are included.** The downloads contain no ISO, ROM, disc data, game art, models, sounds or music. You need your own legally owned copy of *Mortal Kombat: Deception* for PlayStation 2 and a disc image you made from it yourself. Everything game-related is created on your PC from your disc and never leaves it.

## Download

Get the latest test build from [Releases](../../releases): download `MKD-Deception-VR-<version>-win64.zip`, extract it, and double-click **`Mortal Kombat Deception VR.cmd`**. Then **LOAD ISO**, **DeKrypt** (once, a few minutes) and **FIGHT!**

The launcher checks this page for updates (menu → Check for updates) and keeps your prepared data, settings and saves.

## What's in the test build

- Arcade arena fights on all 22 arenas, Versus (local; online is experimental), every fighter's specials, all fighters unlocked.
- Chess Kombat and Puzzle Kombat.
- Konquest (work in progress) and the Krypt (work in progress).

See each release's notes for details and known issues.
